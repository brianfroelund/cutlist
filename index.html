cutlist — free cut list optimizer for woodworking

Plan your cuts before you pick up the saw. List the boards you have and the parts you need, set the saw kerf, and cutlist works out how to cut every part with the least waste. Print a clean cutting plan with a parts list and tick off cuts in the shop.

  • Linear (1D) cutting optimizer for lumber, boards, battens and trim
  • Several stock lengths and materials in one project
  • Kerf-aware; shows offcuts and waste
  • Rough-cut to an ideal planing length, plane, then cut to final length
  • Millimetres or inches (fractions welcome); works on phone and laptop, even offline
