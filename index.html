cutlist — free cut list optimizer for woodworking

Plan your cuts before you pick up the saw. List the boards and sheet goods you have and the parts you need, set the saw kerf, and cutlist works out how to cut every part with the least waste. Print a clean cutting plan with a parts list and tick off cuts in the shop.

  • Linear (1D) cutting optimizer for lumber, boards, battens and trim
  • 2D sheet goods optimizer for plywood, MDF and panels with grain direction and edge trim
  • Guillotine through-cuts for table and track saws plus nested layouts for CNC routers
  • Several stock lengths, sheet sizes and materials in one project
  • Kerf-aware calculation showing usable offcuts and total waste percentage
  • Rough-cut to an ideal planing length, plane, then cut to final length
  • Millimetres or inches (fractions welcome); works on phone and laptop, even offline

Frequently Asked Questions

Is cutlist free?
Yes, cutlist is completely free, open source, and has no ads, subscription fees, or part limits. It works entirely in your browser and works offline.
Does cutlist support both linear lumber and plywood sheet goods?
Yes. You can optimize 1D dimensional lumber boards as well as 2D sheet goods (plywood, MDF, OSB, panels) with multiple stock sizes in a single project.
Does it calculate saw kerf and edge trim?
Yes. Every cut accounts for your saw blade kerf width (e.g. 3 mm or 1/8 inch), and you can specify perimeter edge trim for factory sheet edges.
Can it produce cutting diagrams for table saws and track saws?
Yes. Guillotine cutting mode ensures edge-to-edge through-cuts suitable for table saws and track saws, while nested mode optimizes freeform packing for CNC routers.
Does it support grain direction?
Yes. Parts can be locked along the wood grain, across the grain, or set to any orientation to maximize material yield.
